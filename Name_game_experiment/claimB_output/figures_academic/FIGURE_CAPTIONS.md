## Figure 1. `claimB_primary`

**Partner framing shapes convention abandonment differently in Claude Opus 4.8
and GPT-5.4.**

Proportion of post-change rounds (rounds 8–14) on which a player chose one of
the newly higher-paying letters (M or N) rather than the established
convention (J). Open circles are the 10 independent games per cell, each the
mean of its six players, jittered horizontally; filled circles are condition
means and error bars are 95% game-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals.
Players interact within a game and converge as a unit (ICC of player within
game = .92–.99), so the game, not the player trajectory, is the independent
unit; *n* = 10 games per model × condition. Lines connect condition means
within a model and do not imply pairing across conditions. **(A)** Claude Opus
4.8 rises monotonically across the three framings (Human 0.000, Other LLM
0.567, Self 0.995); all three planned pairwise contrasts remain significant
after Holm correction (Self − Human and Self − Other LLM, *p* < .0001;
Other LLM − Human, *p* = .003). **(B)** GPT-5.4 separates Self (1.000) from
both non-self framings (Human 0.329, Other LLM 0.279; both *p* < .0001), while
Human versus Other LLM did not establish a difference (Holm-adjusted
*p* = .75). Confirmatory inference throughout uses exact game-level
permutation tests on game means.

## Figure 2. `claimB_interaction`

**The effect of partner framing depends on the model.**

Condition means with 95% game-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals, plotted
on a common scale; models are distinguished by line type and marker shape.
Claude Opus 4.8 increases across all three partner categories, whereas GPT-5.4
is flat from Human to Other LLM and rises only at Self. The framing × model
interaction is reliable across the planned contrasts: on the game-level DV
scale, Other LLM − Human is +0.567 for Claude Opus 4.8 and −0.050 for GPT-5.4.
Because the pattern differs by model, the three-way partner distinction should
be attributed to Claude Opus 4.8 in this task rather than to language models in
general.

## Figure 3. `claimB_round_dynamics`

**Time course of adoption over the post-change rounds (descriptive).**

Proportion of players choosing M or N at each of rounds 8–14, by partner
framing, for **(A)** Claude Opus 4.8 and **(B)** GPT-5.4. Conditions are
distinguished by line type and marker shape. The aggregate DV in Figure 1
collapses two opposite trajectories: under Other LLM framing, Claude Opus 4.8
climbs from .15 to .70 as games progressively tip to the new convention, while
GPT-5.4 declines from .35 to .20 as attempted transitions fail and revert to J.
Self framing is at or near ceiling from round 8 in both models; Human framing
stays at zero for Claude Opus 4.8 and rises modestly to .40 for GPT-5.4. This
figure is descriptive and is not the basis of any inferential claim;
game-level intervals are omitted because round-level game data are not part of
the analysis outputs and would clutter the panels.

## Figure 4. `claimB_contrasts`

**Planned pairwise contrasts on the game-level primary DV.**

Differences in game-level mean proportion of post-change rounds choosing M or
N, for **(A)** Claude Opus 4.8 and **(B)** GPT-5.4, with 95% game-clustered
bootstrap intervals; the dotted line marks no difference. Point estimates are
the game-level differences reported in the analysis. All three contrasts
exclude zero for Claude Opus 4.8, consistent with behavioural discrimination
among all three partner categories. For GPT-5.4 the two Self contrasts exclude
zero while Other LLM − Human spans it, consistent with self versus non-self
discrimination only; the data did not establish a difference between Human and
Other LLM, and an equivalence test (TOST, ±0.20 DV margin) likewise did not
establish equivalence.

*Note.* The intervals in this figure are bootstrap intervals on the difference
of game means, computed with the same game-clustered resampling scheme used
for the descriptive intervals in the report; they are provided for display.
Confirmatory *p*-values remain the exact game-level permutation tests reported
in the analysis. The anti-conservative trial-level variational-Bayes credible
intervals are deliberately not shown.
