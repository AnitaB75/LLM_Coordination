==============================================================================
  trial-level rows (rounds 8-14): 2520  = 2 models x 3 framings x 10 games x 6 players x 7 rounds

  3a. SEPARATION DIAGNOSIS (conventional logistic, no random effects)
      cell                    coef (logit)         SE
      Opus 4.8:human                -26.57   17377.07  <-- separated
      Opus 4.8:other_llm              0.27       0.10
      Opus 4.8:self                   5.34       0.71
      gpt-5.4:human                  -0.71       0.10
      gpt-5.4:other_llm              -0.95       0.11
      gpt-5.4:self                   26.57   17376.96  <-- separated

      Opus/human is 0/420 and gpt-5.4/self is 420/420: COMPLETE separation.
      Conventional ML coefficients are therefore unbounded and their SEs
      meaningless. We do NOT report them as inference.

  3b. RANDOM-EFFECTS STRUCTURE
      Rounds are nested in players, players in games. ICC(player|game) is
      0.92-0.99 wherever there is variance, so game is the dominant level.
      We fit random intercepts for GAME and for PLAYER-within-game; where
      a cell has no variance the player term is weakly identified and the
      prior carries it (that is the intended, honest behaviour).

  3c. BAYESIAN MIXED LOGISTIC (statsmodels BinomialBayesMixedGLM, VB)
      mn ~ 0 + C(cell)  +  (1|game) + (1|player)
      Weakly informative N(0, 2.5) priors on the cell logits (fe_p=2.5),
      which is what keeps the separated cells finite.

      cell                    post. mean  post. SD   P(M/N) at RE=0
      Opus 4.8:human              -7.308     0.895            0.001
      Opus 4.8:other_llm          -0.321     0.146            0.420
      Opus 4.8:self                6.266     0.616            0.998
      gpt-5.4:human               -2.042     0.164            0.115
      gpt-5.4:other_llm           -1.465     0.154            0.188
      gpt-5.4:self                 7.829     1.009            1.000

      random-effect SDs (posterior means): game=2.49, player=0.15

      CAVEAT: mean-field VB is known to UNDERSTATE posterior variance, and
      with ICC ~0.92-0.99 the 420 trial rows per cell carry roughly 10
      independent games' worth of information. The trial-level CrIs below
      are therefore anti-conservative; where they disagree with the exact
      game-level permutation test, the permutation test governs.

  gpt-5.4 human      game means sorted: [0.   0.   0.   0.   0.   0.17 0.71 0.76 0.81 0.83]  mean=0.329 median=0.083
  gpt-5.4 other_llm  game means sorted: [0.   0.05 0.05 0.05 0.05 0.19 0.26 0.38 0.81 0.95]  mean=0.279 median=0.119

  The two cells differ in SHAPE, not level: human has 4 games that fully
  tipped (>=.71) and 6 at ~0, other_llm has 2 high and 8 low-but-nonzero.
  The mixed model reports a CONDITIONAL (median-game) probability, which
  favours other_llm; the game-level MEAN favours human. Both are correct
  answers to different questions, and with n=10 games neither is
  distinguishable from no difference (exact perm p=.75).
  CONCLUSION: for gpt-5.4 we report NO established other_llm/human difference.
